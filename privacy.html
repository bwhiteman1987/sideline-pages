Sideline Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Sideline is a family sports calendar app. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

The short version

Sideline doesn't have its own servers and doesn't operate user accounts. Everything you enter — events, photos, comments, RSVPs — is stored in your own personal iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit framework. When you share a calendar with family members, that data syncs through Apple's iCloud sharing system, directly between your devices and theirs. The developer of Sideline cannot see, access, or download your data — there is no company database it passes through.

Sideline does not use advertising, does not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not sell or share your information with anyone.

Information Sideline handles

When you use Sideline, the following information is created and stored in iCloud (yours, or a family calendar's shared iCloud data, depending on what you enter):

  • Family and schedule information — family names, children's names, team names, and event details (titles, times, locations, notes) that you or someone you've shared your calendar with enters.
  • Photos — photos you choose to add to a team's photo album, along with any comments or likes added to them.
  • RSVP responses — whether you or a family member marked an event as going, not going, or maybe, shown under the display name you choose in the app.
  • Activity updates — a feed of the comments, RSVPs, and photos added by people on your shared calendar, generated automatically from the actions above.
  • Notification delivery information — a device token used only to deliver push notifications (for example, when someone RSVPs to an event), handled through Apple's standard push notification service.

Sideline does not require you to create a username or password. Your identity for syncing and sharing is your own Apple ID / iCloud account, which Sideline never sees directly.

Children's information

Sideline is designed for parents and guardians to coordinate their children's activity schedules. Any information about a child (such as a name or a photo) is entered by the parent or guardian using the app for their own family's personal use — it is not collected directly from children, and Sideline is not directed at children as its primary audience. This information is not used for advertising or marketing and is not shared outside the family calendar you choose to share it with.

Where your information is stored

All data is stored using Apple's CloudKit service, inside either:

  • your own private iCloud database (for a family you haven't shared), or
  • a shared CloudKit database, limited to the specific family members you've explicitly invited.

Apple's own privacy and security practices govern how this data is encrypted and stored. Sideline's developer has no separate copy of this data and no way to access it directly.

Your choices and control

  • You can delete any event, photo, or comment you've added directly within the app.
  • You can stop sharing a family calendar at any time, which stops further syncing to the devices you remove.
  • Because there's no Sideline account, there's nothing to "delete" beyond the content itself and leaving any shared calendars — removing the app and your iCloud data removes everything associated with your use of Sideline.
